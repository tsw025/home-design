Same rooms, same ground floor plan. Only the roof changes, so you can see what happens to the tall blind wall on grid 06 and to bedroom 01.

Roof options

Natural ventilation

  • Cool air comes in low, through the doors facing the paddy fields and the three courtyards. Turn on Air flow to see the paths.
  • Glass louvres above the courtyard doors keep air moving when the doors are shut or locked. The drawings' key already lists Breezway-type louvres.
  • Warm air rises and leaves through high openings: the open courtyards in every option, plus the vented band at grid 05 in options B and C.
  • In that band, long, slim windows alternate with vertical slot vents. One concrete fin above them keeps the rain off.
  • There are no openings in the blind wall. Building rules usually don't allow them on a boundary wall, so check this with your architect.
